Buy sustainable polo T-shirts and T-shirts for men online in India

Greylongg makes everyday menswear from fabrics that are kinder to wear and kinder to make. Our polo T-shirts for men come in plain, printed and tipped-collar styles, cut to sit cleanly at the shoulder and stay neat through the day. Our T-shirts for men cover plain round necks for daily wear, and our graphic T-shirts range from quote and gym prints to zodiac and vintage city designs.

Choose by fabric if that is what matters most to you. Bamboo polo T-shirts and bamboo T-shirts are soft and breathable, made for long, warm days. Recycled polo T-shirts use yarn made from recycled material. Organic cotton polos and organic cotton T-shirts are for anyone who prefers a classic cotton feel, and our quick dry, anti-wet T-shirts are built for commutes, travel and the gym.

Buying for more than one? Polo and T-shirt combos bring the price per piece down, and tee and perfume gift sets are ready to give. Our perfumes for men round off the range. For teams, corporate polo T-shirts can be customised with your logo and colours.

Every order is delivered across India, with cash on delivery and an easy exchange if the size is not right.
